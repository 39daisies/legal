Quope Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

39 Daisies ("we," "us") operates the Quope app. This policy explains what data Quope collects and how it's used.

Data We Collect

By default, Quope stores your quotes, journal entries, manifestations, moods, voice recordings, and vision boards locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to or stored on our servers unless you choose to sign in, as described below.

Quope requests camera access to let you capture photos for your Vision boards, and microphone access to let you record voice affirmations in the "Your Voice" feature. These permissions are used only for the features you actively choose to use.

If you subscribe to Quope Premium, payment is processed entirely by Apple through Apple's standard in-app purchase system. We do not collect or store your payment information. Signing in is never required to subscribe or use a free trial.

Optional Sign In with Apple and Cloud Backup

Quope offers an optional "Sign in with Apple" feature so your data can be backed up and made available across your devices. This is entirely optional — you can use every feature of Quope, including subscribing, without ever signing in.

If you choose to sign in, we collect the email address provided by Apple (which may be a private relay address if you choose to hide your email) and use it solely to identify your account. Your app content — manifestations, journal entries, visions, intentions, and voice recordings — is then securely stored with our database provider, Supabase, so it can sync across your devices.

You can stop syncing and delete your cloud-stored data at any time from Settings within the app. Deleting your account removes your data from our servers; content still stored locally on your device is unaffected unless you also delete the app.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Data you choose to sync is used only to provide the backup/sync feature itself.

Your Data

If you have not signed in, your content is stored only locally on your device — deleting the app deletes your data, and we have no way to recover it. If you have signed in, your data also exists in our secure cloud storage until you delete your account or request deletion.

Children's Privacy

Quope is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
