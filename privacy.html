Quope Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

39 Daisies ("we," "us") operates the Quope app. This policy explains what data Quope collects and how it's used.

Data We Collect

Quope stores your quotes, journal entries, manifestations, moods, voice recordings, and vision boards locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Quope requests camera access to let you capture photos for your Vision boards, and microphone access to let you record voice affirmations in the "Your Voice" feature. These permissions are used only for the features you actively choose to use.

If you subscribe to Quope Premium, payment is processed entirely by Apple/Google through their standard in-app purchase systems. We do not collect or store your payment information.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

Your Data

Since your content is stored locally on your device, deleting the app deletes your data. We do not currently offer cloud backup.

Children's Privacy

Quope is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
