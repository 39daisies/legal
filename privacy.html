Quope Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

39 Daisies ("we," "us") operates the Quope app. This policy explains what data Quope collects and how it's used.

Data We Collect

  • Quope stores your quotes, journal entries, manifestations, moods, voice recordings, and vision boards locally on your device.
  • If you subscribe to Quope Premium and enable cross-device sync, this content is also synced to our database (hosted with Supabase) so it's available across your devices. See "Cross-Device Sync" below.
  • Quope requests camera access to let you capture photos for your Vision boards, and microphone access to let you record voice affirmations in the "Your Voice" feature. These permissions are used only for the features you actively choose to use.
  • If you sign in (for example, with Sign in with Apple) to enable sync, we receive and store the email address associated with your sign-in — which may be an Apple-provided private relay address — along with an account identifier.

Cross-Device Sync

  • Cross-device sync is a Premium feature. While your subscription is active, your content is synced across your devices via our database, hosted with Supabase.
  • If your subscription lapses or is canceled, sync stops for any new content going forward. Content already synced as of your cancellation date is not deleted, but will no longer update or sync to additional devices unless Premium is reactivated.
  • Content stored locally on your device remains fully accessible regardless of subscription status.

Account Deletion

  • If you delete your account, we permanently remove your synced data from our servers, including database records and any associated authentication records.
  • Content stored locally on your device is not affected by an account deletion request and remains on your device unless you separately delete the app.

Subscriptions & Billing

  • Quope offers optional Premium subscriptions, purchased through Apple's or Google's standard in-app purchase systems.
  • We use RevenueCat to manage and validate subscription status across devices. RevenueCat receives subscription and purchase-event data (such as your subscription tier and renewal status) necessary to keep your Premium access working correctly. We do not collect or store your payment card information — that is handled entirely by Apple/Google.

Analytics

  • We collect limited, first-party usage analytics (such as app events) to understand how Quope is used and to improve the app. This data is used internally and is not shared with third-party advertising networks.
  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers, and we do not sell your personal data.

Email Communications

  • If you have an account, we may send you occasional emails related to your subscription (such as trial or renewal reminders) or app reminders you've opted into.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, other than the service providers described above (Supabase for data storage, RevenueCat for subscription management) who process data on our behalf to operate the app.

Your Data

  • If you are not a Premium subscriber, or have not enabled sync, your content is stored only locally on your device — deleting the app deletes that data.
  • If you are a Premium subscriber with sync enabled, your content is also stored in our database. You can request deletion of this data at any time by deleting your account (see "Account Deletion" above) or contacting us.

Children's Privacy

  • Quope is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

  • We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
