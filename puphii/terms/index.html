3. What Puphii is, and isn’t

Puphii is a habit-awareness tool. You tap once each time you smoke or vape, and a small pufferfish reacts. You can also set an optional reduction plan, estimate spending, play a short game, record Voice Pep Rallies and open the SOS screen.

Puphii’s reactions are playful feedback. They are not a measurement of physical harm, a diagnosis or medical advice. Puphii is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Plan paces and targets are examples you choose and can change. They are not clinical recommendations.

The SOS screen and the Learn section give general tips and links to public services. They are not medical care, an emergency service or crisis counselling. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (for example 911 in the United States, 000 in Australia or 111 in New Zealand).

If you’re worried about your health, please talk to a healthcare professional.

We don’t hold a copy and can’t restore lost data.

You can delete your data in the app’s Data Management settings. If your subscription has ended, Data Management stays available from the subscription screen without unlocking the rest of the app. You can also delete the app. The Privacy Policy describes what each option removes, and how to ask us to delete subscription records held by our subscription provider.
