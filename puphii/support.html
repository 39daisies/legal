Manage or cancel a subscription

Puphii Pro is billed by Apple through your Apple Account. To manage or cancel:

  • In the Puphii app, open You → Subscription, then Manage Subscription, or
  • On iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or
  • Open Apple’s subscriptions page: apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
