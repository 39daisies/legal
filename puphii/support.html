Support

We’re here to help with anything you run into.

Reach us directly at puphiicount@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 1–2 business days. In the app, open You → Contact Us to start an email with your app version already filled in. For common questions, open You → Customer Support.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account to use Puphii? No. Puphii has no account or cloud login. Your logs, plan, settings and Voice Pep Rallies stay on your iPhone.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Puphii → You → Subscription, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. In Puphii: You → Subscription → Manage Subscription. Or on iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Or open Apple’s page: apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • How do I get a refund? App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • How do I delete my data? In Puphii, open You → Data Management and use Reset everything to clear logs, plan, settings and on-device Voice Pep Rallies on that iPhone. Uninstalling Puphii also removes data stored only on that device. App Store subscriptions are separate and stay in your Apple ID settings until you cancel them.
  • Is Puphii emergency or clinical care? No. Puphii is a habit tool with optional Quick support tips. If you are in immediate danger or having thoughts of self-harm, contact local emergency services or a crisis line in your country.

Something else?

Email us at puphiicount@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you.
