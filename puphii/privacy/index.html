  • Puphii has no account. Your puff and skip logs, plan, settings, savings calculator details, Bubble Break scores and Voice Pep Rallies stay on your device. We never receive them.

4. Deleting app data

Because the app’s information stays on your device, you control it there. In Puphii’s settings, open Data Management. If your subscription has ended, Data Management stays available from the subscription screen without unlocking the rest of the app:

  • Clear puff history permanently deletes your puff logs, along with your plan’s milestones and the days you confirmed as puff-free. Skip logs, Voice Pep Rallies, plan settings, other settings, savings calculator details and Bubble Break scores stay.
  • Reset everything returns your puff and skip logs, plan, settings, savings calculator, Bubble Break record and Voice Pep Rallies to their defaults (and deletes Pep Rally audio files) and turns reminders off. It keeps only the date the app was first opened and its data format version. It doesn’t affect a Puphii Pro subscription or the RevenueCat customer ID (section 6).
  • Deleting the app removes everything Puphii stored on your device.

We don’t hold a copy of this information, so we can’t recover it after deletion.

5. Reminders

Daily reminders are optional. Puphii asks for notification permission only when you turn reminders on. Reminders are scheduled on your device, and nothing is sent to a server. The reminder text doesn’t mention smoking or vaping.

6. Puphii Pro subscriptions

Puphii Pro subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple through your Apple Account. Puphii never sees your card or payment details.

Puphii uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States) to check whether you have Puphii Pro, show subscription prices and free-trial eligibility, and restore purchases. Puphii connects to RevenueCat when the app starts, from time to time while you use it, and when you buy or restore a purchase. This happens whether or not you subscribe, because the app needs to know whether access is unlocked.

What RevenueCat receives

RevenueCat receives a random customer ID, the vendor identifier (IDFV) that iOS gives apps from the same developer, your device model, iOS version, app version and build, language settings, App Store country, tracking-permission status (not determined, because Puphii never asks), and your purchase and subscription information.

Like any internet connection, the connection reveals your IP address to RevenueCat. RevenueCat’s documentation says it uses the IP address to estimate your country when the App Store country isn’t available, and does not store the IP address.

We see customer records in RevenueCat’s dashboard (customer ID, purchases, last-seen time, app and iOS version, country) and totals. They do not include your name or email address. These identifiers stay the same while Puphii is installed, so the information is pseudonymous, not anonymous.

What RevenueCat does not receive

  • Your puff logs, skip logs, plan, savings details, settings, game scores or Voice Pep Rally audio. They stay on your device.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA). Puphii does not collect it, does not ask for tracking permission, and does not share data with advertising or attribution services.
  • Puphii does not use RevenueCat’s paywall, advertising or attribution features.

Why

To provide the subscription (show what can be bought, confirm what you bought, and give you access) and to see overall subscription totals. The information is not used for advertising or to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. RevenueCat processes this information as our service provider, under its data processing agreement with us. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy. RevenueCat uses sub-processors, all listed in its Data Processing Addendum.

Where

RevenueCat says it stores this information on Amazon Web Services servers in the United States. Its privacy policy says information may also be processed in other countries where it or its service providers operate.

Cancelling and restoring

You can manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone’s Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple’s guide, Cancel a subscription from Apple. Deleting Puphii doesn’t cancel a subscription.

Restore Purchases, on the subscription screen, restores access to an active subscription bought with the same Apple Account (Terms of Use, section 5.6).

Deleting subscription records

Deleting Puphii removes the customer ID from your device, but not the records RevenueCat holds. To ask us to delete them, email puphiicount@gmail.com. Puphii has no accounts and the app doesn’t display the customer ID, so we need your help to find your records:

  • If you bought a subscription, include the order ID shown on your receipt from Apple and the approximate purchase date. We use them to look for your records.
  • If you never bought a subscription, your records contain no purchase history and are linked only to the random ID on your device. We usually can’t find them, so we may not be able to act on the request.

Please don’t send card details, passwords or your Apple Account sign-in details. We will tell you what we found and what we did.

When we find your records, we delete them through RevenueCat. RevenueCat describes deletion as permanent and completes it in the background. Its privacy policy says it may keep personal information under its retention policy where needed to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce its agreements, and its data processing agreement refers to its standard backup and archival practices.

Deleting these records doesn’t cancel your subscription, so cancel it with Apple first. If you keep using Puphii, the app creates a new record at RevenueCat the next time it checks your subscription.

7. Links to other websites and phone numbers

The app’s Learn section links to public health sources such as cdc.gov, smokefree.gov and nhs.uk. The SOS screen links to quit-support, crisis and emergency resources for the United States, Australia and New Zealand, and may open phone numbers (for example 911, 000 or 111) with your device’s dialer. These open in your browser or phone app only when you tap them. The site or phone service receives a normal request (such as an IP address and browser details for websites, which may set cookies under their own policies). Puphii doesn’t add any of your information to the link.

8. No advertising or tracking

Puphii has no advertising, attribution, usage-analytics or crash-reporting services of its own, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not sell personal information. The only information we receive about how the app is used is the subscription information in section 6 and, if you opt in on your device, Apple’s reports (section 3).

Because we don’t track visitors across websites, we don’t treat browser “Do Not Track” signals differently.

Canva hosts the website at puphii.com. Canva is based in Australia. Canva’s delivery may involve other infrastructure providers, including Cloudflare, which is based in the United States. The poll page at polls.puphii.com runs through ChatGPT Sites, provided by OpenAI, which is based in the United States, and its infrastructure, including Cloudflare. When you visit the poll page, those providers can receive your IP address and may process browser or device information, request times, requested pages, cookies, and security or operational logs to deliver, protect and operate the page.

We have not added separate advertising pixels, fingerprinting or cross-site analytics of our own. That is not a claim that Canva’s or ChatGPT Sites’ platforms are cookie-free. For example, security cookies may be set by the hosting stack. Their collection and retention are governed by their services and privacy practices, and we do not independently control every provider log.

More information: Canva Privacy Policy, OpenAI Privacy Policy and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

9.2 Optional polls (polls.puphii.com)

The poll page asks whether quitting or cutting back is on your mind and what kind of encouragement you would like. These topics may be personal. Answering is optional, and no name or email address is requested. When you submit an answer, the selected option is sent to a server that adds it to a daily total. The application stores only a date-and-option key and a count. It also keeps daily totals of page loads and poll interactions. It does not store a visitor ID, an IP address or an individual response. Totals are shown to visitors. Your browser’s session storage remembers what you submitted in that tab to discourage repeat submissions; it is not sent as an identifier. Hosting providers may still process IP addresses and technical logs (section 9.1). Because no individual response is stored, we cannot find or delete a specific person’s vote.

16. Children

Puphii relates to smoking and vaping. It is intended only for people aged 18 or older. The App Store age rating for Puphii is 18+. On devices that support it, the app asks Apple’s Declared Age Range service for your age category and does not store the answer or send it to us or anyone else. If Apple says you are under 18, the app does not open. Where Apple does not provide this signal, Puphii asks you to confirm that you are 18 or older. Confirming stores only a local confirmation flag on your device—not your age or date of birth. Saying you are under 18 blocks the app for that session and is not saved. Puphii doesn’t collect your name, email address or other contact details through the app for age checks.

17. Educational scope

The app and website are not medical advice, a dependence test, or a treatment plan. We make no health inference from your answers or logs. Puphii’s reactions are playful feedback, not a measurement of physical harm or a diagnosis.
