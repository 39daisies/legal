Common questions

  • Do I need an account to use Quope? No. Quotes, journal, visions, and the 369 Method all work without signing in. Sign in with Apple is optional — it backs up your progress so you can pick up on another device.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Quope, go to the You tab, and tap Restore purchases near the bottom of the screen.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Or open Quope → You → Manage subscription.
  • How do I delete my data? If you are signed in with Apple, open Quope → You → Delete account. That removes your cloud backup and clears data on this phone. If you are not signed in, uninstalling Quope removes all locally stored data on this device. App Store subscriptions are managed separately in Apple ID settings.
