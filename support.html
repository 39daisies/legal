Common questions

  • Do I need an account to use Quope? No. Everything in Quope works without an account. Your content is stored on your iPhone, and iCloud backup (free and on by default) keeps a copy in your own iCloud account. You can turn it off in Quope → You → iCloud backup.
  • How do I move to a new iPhone? Sign in to the new iPhone with the same Apple ID, install Quope, and tap Restore from iCloud on the first screen.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Quope, go to the You tab, and tap Restore purchases near the bottom of the screen.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Or open Quope → You → Manage subscription.
  • How do I delete my data? Deleting Quope removes the data on that iPhone. To delete your iCloud backup, open Quope → You and turn off iCloud backup, then tap Turn off and delete backup. You can also delete it in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Storage → Quope. If you signed in with Apple in an earlier version, open Quope → You → Delete account. That removes your account, your iCloud backup, and the data on that iPhone. App Store subscriptions are managed separately in Apple ID settings.
