Common questions

  • Do I need an account to use Quope? No. Every core feature — quotes, journal, visions, and the 369 Method — works without signing in. Signing in with Apple is optional and only enables backup across devices.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Quope, go to the You tab, and tap "Restore Purchases" near the bottom of the screen.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed entirely through your Apple ID. Go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone to manage or cancel.
  • How do I delete my data? If you've signed in with Apple, you can delete your synced data any time from the You tab. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
